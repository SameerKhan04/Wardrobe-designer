Privacy policy

Wardrobe · Android · 14 October 2026

Wardrobe is a closet on your phone. Photos and outfit notes stay on the device unless you create an account. The app does not show ads, and it does not sell your information.

Who this covers

This policy describes the Wardrobe mobile app, Android package com.sameerkhan.wardrobe. It applies when you install the app from Google Play or from a copy the developer gives you.

What stays on your phone

Without an account, Wardrobe does not send your closet anywhere. The app stores, on the device only:

  • Photos of clothes you take or pick, including the cutout version
  • Details you set for each piece: category, colour, formality, warmth, rain, breathability, and whether it is clean, in the laundry, or at the dry cleaner
  • Saved looks, including any name and tags such as Work or Casual
  • The last temperature and rain setting you used for suggestions

Uninstalling the app removes this on-device copy.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to photograph clothes. Choosing a photo uses Android’s photo picker, which does not grant the app broad access to your gallery. Photos are not used to identify you, and they are not used for advertising.

Cutting a garment out of the background runs on the phone with an on-device model (ML Kit). The photo is not uploaded for that step. The first time, the phone may download the model. If a cutout fails, the original photo is kept.

Location and weather

Location is off until you tap Use where I am on the Today screen. The app then asks for foreground location, reads it once, and does not track you in the background.

That one reading is sent to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) to fetch the temperature and the chance of rain. Open-Meteo does not require an account. Wardrobe does not store the coordinates. It keeps only the temperature and a rain yes/no on the phone so the screen can reopen where you left it. You can ignore location and set the temperature and rain yourself.

Optional account

You can use Wardrobe with no account. If you sign in, the app creates an account with your email and password and syncs your closet so another phone signed into the same account can show it.

That sync uses Supabase:

  • Email and password are handled by Supabase Authentication
  • Garment details and saved looks are stored in a database row that only your account can read or change
  • Photos are stored in a private folder named with your account id. They are not public

Sync runs when you sign in, after you edit something, and when you bring the app back to the front. If the network is down, the phone copy keeps working and catches up later. Signing out stops sync on that phone. It does not delete the cloud copy.

Deleting your information

Delete a piece or a saved look inside the app and it is removed from the phone. If you are signed in, that deletion syncs to your account.

If you are signed in, You → Delete my account permanently deletes the cloud closet, the photos in your private folder, the account, and the closet on that phone. This cannot be undone.

You can also ask for deletion by opening an issue on the Wardrobe repository. Use the same email as the account if you have one, and do not include photos of other people.

What this app does not do

  • No advertising
  • No sale of personal information
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK
  • No background location
  • No microphone recording
  • No access to your whole photo library

The developer does not use your clothes, looks, email, or location to build an advertising profile.

Children

Wardrobe is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact the developer and it will be deleted.

Security

Cloud traffic uses HTTPS. Account data is limited to the signed-in user. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Keep your password to yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the app after the new date means the updated policy applies.

Contact
