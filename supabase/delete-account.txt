Delete your Wardrobe account

This page is for the Android app Wardrobe. You can ask for your account to be deleted from here. You do not need the app installed.

How to request deletion

1. Send an email to skha0730@uni.sydney.edu.au
2. Use the subject "Delete my Wardrobe account"
3. Send it from the email address on the account, and include that address in the message.

If you still have Wardrobe installed, you can delete it yourself instead: open the app, go to You, tap Delete my account, then Delete everything. That finishes immediately.

What is deleted

The account and the data stored with it are deleted, and nothing is kept afterwards:

- Your email address and login
- Your clothes (category, colour, and the other details you entered)
- Your saved looks
- Photos of your clothes stored with the account

Email requests are completed within 7 days. There is no extra retention period.

Location is not saved with your account. It is used only when you tap "Use where I am", to fetch the weather, and then discarded. A copy of your closet can remain on a phone until you uninstall Wardrobe or delete the account from that phone.

Delete some data and keep the account

In the app, remove a garment or a saved look and that item, including its photo, is deleted. Your account stays.

To ask for the same thing without the app, email skha0730@uni.sydney.edu.au with the subject "Delete some of my Wardrobe data", from the account email, and say which clothes, looks, or photos to remove.
